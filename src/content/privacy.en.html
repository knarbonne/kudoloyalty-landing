1. Who is responsible for your data

Kudo is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], registered in Thailand under company number [REGISTRATION NO.], at [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA"), responsibility splits depending on whose data it is:

  • For merchant account data, Kudo is the data controller.
  • For member data, the merchant running the loyalty programme is the data controller and Kudo is the data processor. We handle member data on that merchant's instructions. If you are a member and want your data deleted, the merchant decides, though you can contact us and we will pass the request on.

2. What we collect

From merchants

  • Name, email address, and the business details you enter during setup
  • Your LINE Official Account identifier and the credentials needed to send messages on your behalf
  • Billing records for paid plans
  • Support correspondence

From members

  • Your LINE user ID, display name, and profile image, received from LINE when you join a programme
  • Your date of birth, only if you provide it, and only so the merchant can send birthday rewards
  • Your points or stamp balance, and the history of how you earned and redeemed them
  • Which rewards you claimed, and when
  • Activity used to group you into segments such as New, Active, At risk or Lapsed

We do not receive your LINE password, your LINE chat history with anyone else, your phone number, or your friend list.

Collected automatically

  • IP address, browser and device type, and pages viewed
  • Cookies and similar storage strictly needed to keep you signed in and remember your language choice

4. How LINE fits in

Kudo runs inside LINE, so LINE Corporation is involved in most of what happens:

  • When you join a programme, you authorise LINE to share your user ID, display name and profile image with the merchant through Kudo.
  • Messages a merchant sends you, whether broadcasts, win-backs or birthday rewards, are delivered by LINE's Messaging API and are subject to LINE's own terms and privacy policy.
  • You can block or unfriend a merchant's LINE Official Account at any time to stop receiving messages. That does not delete your points; to do that, contact the merchant.

LINE's handling of your data is governed by LINE's privacy policy, not this one.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct it if it is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete it, or restrict how we use it
  • Transfer it to another provider in a machine-readable form
  • Stop processing it, where we rely on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw a consent you previously gave. This does not undo processing that already happened

Email krissada.narbonne@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are a member, we will forward your request to the merchant who controls your data, and support them in answering it.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you may complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access to production data is limited to people who need it to operate or support the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify affected users and the PDPC of a qualifying breach as the PDPA requires.
